# Example Bank: Angel / Not Angel

Calibration pairs across surfaces. Each pair says the same thing twice: how
Angel sounds, and the trap to avoid. Use these to tune your ear before
writing, or as before/after material when rewriting existing copy.

## Show promotion

**Angel:** "The shepherd who became king. New episodes every Sunday."
**Not Angel:** "An epic saga of faith, destiny, and power. Don't miss it."
*Specific subject and concrete cadence vs. three abstract nouns, no
receipts, no subject.*

**Angel:** "From the creators of David. In theaters June 12."
**Not Angel:** "The most anticipated faith film of the year is almost here!"
*Named proof and a date vs. unverifiable hype and an exclamation point.*

**Angel:** "From the creators of David."
**Not Angel:** "From the studio that brought you The Chosen."
*Named proof must come from Angel's own titles. The series "The Chosen"
(formerly on Angel, no longer on the platform) is legally off-limits in
all Angel copy, with no exceptions.*

## Guild & membership

**Angel:** "Join Guild for $20/mo. You fund the next season."
**Not Angel:** "Unlock an unparalleled premium viewing experience today!"
*Price, cadence, and the member's role vs. jargon and euphemism for
paying us.*

**Angel:** "Guild vote closes Sunday: pick the next pilot."
**Not Angel:** "Last chance to make your voice heard. Act now!"
*A real deadline and a real action vs. manufactured urgency.*

## Pay It Forward

**Angel:** "Watch free. Pay it forward when you're able. It's how the next
family gets in."
**Not Angel:** "Support impactful content with a generous contribution today."
*Names the mechanic and why it matters vs. vague ask, vague subject, vague
benefit.*

**Angel:** "Your gift landed. Someone's watching free tonight."
**Not Angel:** "Thank you for your generous support of our mission!"
*A specific receipt vs. a form letter.*

## Brand & mission statements

**Angel:** "Stories that inspire hope and strengthen families."
**Not Angel:** "Stories that shimmer like constellations across the human
spirit."
*Clear and direct vs. flowery metaphor. Clarity is more important than
cleverness; poetic decoration hides the subject.*

## Punctuation

**Angel:** "Payment updated. You're all set through March."
**Not Angel:** "Payment updated — you're all set through March."
*Same words, wrong joint. Em dashes are banned in Angel copy, even where
one would normally be stylistically fine. Use a period, comma, or
parentheses, or restructure.*

## Errors

**Angel:**
> **Your Guild Membership Is Safe**
> We couldn't process your payment because your card was declined. Update
> your card and we'll try again right away.

**Not Angel:**
> **Oops! Something went wrong 😅**
> There was a problem with your payment. Please try again later.

*Reassure → explain → fix, with honest specifics vs. cutesy deflection and
a dead end.*

**Angel:**
> **Let's Get You Back to the Story**
> Playback hit a snag on our end. It usually clears on a retry.

**Not Angel:**
> **Error 4302: Playback failed**
> An unexpected error has occurred.

*Calm ownership and a next step vs. machine-speak with no path forward.*

## Empty states

**Angel:**
> **Your Watchlist Is Waiting**
> Save shows here and they'll be ready when you are.

**Not Angel:**
> **Nothing here yet!**
> You haven't added anything to your watchlist.

*Invitation framing vs. scolding the member for emptiness.*

## Confirmations

**Angel:** "Payment updated. You're all set through March."
**Not Angel:** "Success! Your payment method has been updated successfully!"
*The receipt is the reassurance vs. redundant celebration.*

## Cancellation

**Angel:**
> **Cancel Guild Membership?**
> You'll keep member access through March 14. After that, you'll stop
> funding new seasons and lose early access.

**Not Angel:**
> **We're so sad to see you go 😢**
> Are you SURE you want to abandon the stories that need you most?

*Plain facts including the honest mechanic vs. guilt theatrics.*

## Email subjects

**Angel:** "David S2 premieres Sunday at 8pm"
**Not Angel:** "You won't BELIEVE what's coming this Sunday 👀"

**Angel:** "Your card was declined (2-minute fix)"
**Not Angel:** "Action required: payment failure notification"

*Specific promise vs. bait; human problem-and-fix vs. robo-corporate.*

## Push notifications

**Angel:** "New episode: David S2 E5 is live"
**Not Angel:** "Something new just dropped… 🔥 Open the app!"

## Titles & casing

**Angel:** "Featured Series" *(under 6 words → Title Case, no period)*
**Not Angel:** "Featured Series." *(no title ever ends in a period)*

**Angel:** "Stories worth telling are worth protecting" *(6+ words →
sentence case)*
**Not Angel:** "Stories Worth Telling Are Worth Protecting" *(long titles
in Title Case read as mistakes)*
